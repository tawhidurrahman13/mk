CompTIA  Security+  Practice  Test  3  —  Questions,  Choices  &  Answers  1.  What  is  the  best  prevention  method  against  ransomware  delivered  through  
phishing
 
emails?
 
 
A)  Regular  vulnerability  scanning  
B)  User  training  on  recognizing  phishing  emails  
C)  Deployment  of  an  IPS  
D)  Blocking  all  email  attachments  
 
Correct  Answer:  B)  User  training  on  recognizing  phishing  emails  
 
------------------------------------------------------------  
2.  Which  firewall  inspects  packet  content  at  the  application  layer?  
 
A)  Stateful  firewall  
B)  Packet-filtering  firewall  
C)  Next-generation  firewall  (NGFW)  
D)  Circuit-level  gateway  
 
Correct  Answer:  C)  Next-generation  firewall  (NGFW)  
 
------------------------------------------------------------  
3.  What  is  the  primary  purpose  of  SIEM  solutions?  
 
A)  Encrypt  sensitive  data  
B)  Correlate  and  analyze  security  logs  in  real  time  
C)  Prevent  malware  infections  
D)  Automate  vulnerability  management  
 
Correct  Answer:  B)  Correlate  and  analyze  security  logs  in  real  time  
 
------------------------------------------------------------  
4.  What  is  the  best  mitigation  against  attackers  guessing  weak  passwords?  
 
A)  Password  complexity  policies  
B)  Multi-factor  authentication  (MFA)  
C)  Deploy  a  WAF  
D)  Require  password  changes  every  30  days  
 
Correct  Answer:  B)  Multi-factor  authentication  (MFA)  
 
------------------------------------------------------------  
5.  What  is  the  best  action  after  identifying  a  rogue  device  on  the  network?  
 
A)  Block  the  MAC  address  
B)  Isolate  the  device  using  segmentation  
C)  Deploy  EDR  
D)  Shut  down  the  switch  
 
Correct  Answer:  B)  Isolate  the  device  using  segmentation  
 
------------------------------------------------------------  
6.  Which  cryptographic  method  ensures  authenticity  of  software  updates?  
 
A)  Symmetric  encryption  
B)  Digital  signatures  
C)  MD5  hashing  
D)  ECC  
 
Correct  Answer:  B)  Digital  signatures  
 
------------------------------------------------------------  
7.  What  is  the  best  immediate  response  to  a  DDoS  attack?  
 
A)  Deploy  a  load  balancer  
B)  Block  all  incoming  traffic  
C)  Redirect  traffic  through  a  DDoS  mitigation  service  
D)  Notify  customers  
 
Correct  Answer:  C)  Redirect  traffic  through  a  DDoS  mitigation  service  
 
------------------------------------------------------------  
8.  What  is  the  purpose  of  a  honeypot?  
 
A)  Divert  attackers  and  gather  intelligence  
B)  Encrypt  sensitive  data  
C)  Prevent  brute-force  attacks  
D)  Analyze  legitimate  users  
 
Correct  Answer:  A)  Divert  attackers  and  gather  intelligence  
 
------------------------------------------------------------  
9.  Which  protocol  ensures  confidentiality  and  integrity  of  email  communications?  
 
A)  S/MIME  
B)  SMTP  
C)  IMAP  
D)  POP3  
 
Correct  Answer:  A)  S/MIME  
 
------------------------------------------------------------  
10.  What  type  of  attack  repeatedly  tries  default  usernames  and  passwords  on  IoT  
devices?
 
 
A)  Brute  force  
B)  Credential  stuffing  
C)  Password  spraying  
D)  Dictionary  attack  
 
Correct  Answer:  A)  Brute  force

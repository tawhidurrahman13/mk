Risk  Management  -  Unit  5  Final  Question  Bank  1.  Contracting  out  a  specialized  technical  component  is  an  example  of:  
 
A.  Risk  acceptance  
 
B.  Risk  transference  
 
C.  Risk  deterrence  
 
D.  Risk  avoidance  
 
Correct  Answer:  B.  Risk  transference

B.  $500,000  
 
C.  $20,000  
 
D.  $5,000  
 
Correct  Answer:  A.  $100,000

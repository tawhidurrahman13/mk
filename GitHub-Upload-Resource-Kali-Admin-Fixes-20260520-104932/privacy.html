Student privacy and records handling

SOC Bootcamp Privacy Notice

This notice explains how this training app is designed to limit and protect student account, quiz, certification, and practice exam data.

Data collected

Minimum Training Records

The app uses account email, display name, role, quiz attempts, practice exam scores, certification status, and Kali guide progress only for training and admin support.

Access

Role-Based Visibility

Students should only access their own records. Admin views are limited to authorized accounts and are logged when student records are viewed or changed.

Security

Protected Transport

Production deployments must use HTTPS, secure cookies, MFA, CSRF protections, and server-side authorization before real student records are used.

Review needed

School Approval

Use with real student education records requires school/district legal, privacy, and IT approval, including retention, deletion, access, amendment, vendor, and AI-use policies.

AI and third-party systems

Do not paste real student names, emails, IDs, education records, answers, grades, or screenshots into AI tools or analytics unless the school has approved that vendor and workflow for FERPA-aligned use.

Student requests

Requests to inspect, correct, export, retain, or delete student records should follow the school’s official student records process. This page is not legal advice.
